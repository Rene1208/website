<a id="about-me"></a>
I am a Postdoctoral Researcher and Lecturer in Experimental Economics, Finance, and Metascience at the Department of
Banking and Finance, University of Innsbruck. I also serve as Scientific Coordinator of Lab Operations at the Innsbruck
EconLab.

My research examines judgment and decision-making under risk and uncertainty, sustainable and socially responsible
choices, and the production of credible evidence through large-scale crowd science and metascience. I combine laboratory,
online, field, and cross-country experiments with rigorous empirical analysis.

Research Program
======
<a id="research-interests"></a>

My work spans three connected research areas:

- **Judgment and Decision-Making Under Risk and Uncertainty:** Studying framing, investment behavior, and the cognitive
  mechanisms underlying financial decisions.
- **Sustainable and Socially Responsible Choices:** Examining environmental policy support, collective action, green
  investment, and pro-environmental behavior.
- **Crowd Science and Metascience:** Coordinating many-designs, many-analyst, multi-lab, and cross-country studies to
  improve the robustness, transparency, and cumulative value of scientific evidence.

Current Research
======
<a id="publications-talks"></a>

My recent work includes *Fifty-five Crowd-Sourced Designs Assessing Carbon Pricing Support*, accepted for publication at
*Nature Human Behaviour*. I am joint first author and a member of the five-person core organizing team for this project,
which evaluated 55 experimental designs with 19,558 participants. Other recent publications appear in the *Proceedings
of the National Academy of Sciences* and *Management Science*.

Current manuscripts examine visible tax dividends, decision flexibility and myopic loss aversion, cognitive fatigue in
laboratory research, and virtual nature in open-world games. Full details are available on the [publications](/publications/),
[working papers](/working-papers/), and [talks](/talks/) pages.

Teaching and Mentoring
======
<a id="teaching-mentoring"></a>

I teach and supervise across undergraduate, graduate, MBA, and professional programs in finance, economics,
sustainability, and ethical decision-making. My teaching portfolio includes 32 official reports and 332 student
questionnaires. Across the 27 reports containing global scores, average ratings are 1.29 for content and 1.38 for delivery
on a five-point scale where 1 is the best score. More information is available on the [teaching](/teaching/) page.

Academic Engagement
======

I contribute to the FAIRInv consortium on responsible investment, funded by the French National Research Agency with
EUR 4.0 million for 2026-2030, and served as a contributing author to the Austrian Assessment Report on Climate Change 2.
My academic service includes the University of Innsbruck Curriculum Committee, departmental research meetings, student
mentoring, and conference organization. I received the 2023 Clemens August Andreae Prize for the best dissertation in
economics at the University of Innsbruck.
